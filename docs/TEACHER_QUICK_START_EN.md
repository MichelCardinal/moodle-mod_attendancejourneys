# Getting started with Attendance Journeys

## Prepare meetings

Add Attendance Journeys to the course. Light mode creates a main journey with routine recording and individual closure; Professional mode adds independent journeys, rooms and advanced operations. Check course enrolments and each session's group or journey audience.

In Sessions, add the meeting name and its start and end times. Professional mode can prepare a series: review its occurrences before confirmation. Each journey brings together the sessions and participants of one obligation.

## Reuse an organisation

Duplicate a session, then adjust its date and audience. To reuse several sessions, select them and use schedule duplication: choose the date offset, retain or change the audience and review the preview. Attendance is not copied. Protected sessions may be excluded; the screen explains why.

To reuse activity structure in another course, use Moodle backup/restore without user data. Journeys and sessions are retained; enrolments and attendance must be created for the new course.

## Record attendance

Choose the session in Take attendance. Use Present, Absent, Partial or Excused as enabled. Partial uses minutes absent. Review the calculated time, add a useful remark if needed and save.

In new activities, Excused remains absence for calculation; an authorised session exemption removes that obligation. Unrecorded remains pending. Historical activities retain their policy until an authorised conversion.

Sheets display up to 100 participants per page. Search, filters and status-preparation buttons concern displayed rows on that page. The approval button covers all declarations on the page, including filtered-out declarations. Save before changing pages and repeat on subsequent pages.

If the sheet changed while you were editing, review its current state before resuming: another staff member or an administrative action may have changed it.

## Review a self-declaration

When enabled, eligible students can declare attendance. Review before approval. Request a correction with an explanation when needed; the student can resubmit. Approved or staff-recorded attendance cannot be freely edited by the student.

## Finalise and retain results

Review collective and individual reports. Resolve missing entries, pending declarations and equivalences before closing. Closure freezes the configured result; reopening permits correction while preserving history.

Each independent obligation publishes its grade only after closure. Future or ongoing sessions prevent closure. Required automatic completion needs all assigned required obligations to be closed and passed. Reopening withdraws only the selected final result, subject to Moodle gradebook protections.

Choose an export format and check the selected journey and group before sharing it. Report problems using fictional data and the Moodle and plugin versions.
